# Result Reveal Motion Identity

## Style Prompt

Technical documentary motion for a university innovation service center. Real photography and precise data remain dominant. Cyan signal paths and restrained gold highlights explain aggregation and lock-on states without turning the experience into a game interface.

## Colors

- `#07111f`: deep technical canvas
- `#31c7e8`: signal path and progress
- `#f2c14e`: locked result and key number
- `#f7fafc`: primary text
- `#b8c7d8`: supporting text

## Typography

- Chinese: system sans-serif
- Numeric labels: system monospace with tabular numerals

## Timing

- 0.15-0.55s: four signal paths converge
- 0.55-1.05s: coordinate lock and match count
- 1.05-1.60s: documentary image and department title reveal

## What NOT to Do

- No purple-dominant palette
- No glass card stack
- No full-screen particle field
- No continuous high-contrast flashing
- No text hidden after the sequence settles
